The Frame

This pilot is not a housing project. It is the first investment in an environment designed to attract, support, and retain healthcare professionals across Northern BC — starting with Dawson Creek as the anchor and proof of concept for a provincial model.

Layer 1
The Foundation — Accommodations
Everything else is built on top of this. Without a solid accommodation foundation, nothing above it works.
Core accommodation types
🛏 Furnished Short-Term Housing
Locums, agency staff, and rotating healthcare workers. Move-in ready, fully managed, no admin required from NH.
🏠 Long-Term Housing
Permanent staff and families. Stable lease structures that give people a reason to plant roots in the community.
⚙️ Operations & Maintenance
Full day-to-day operations managed by ERS. Every unit maintained to standard, every issue handled — zero NH involvement required.
👥 Staff Coordination
Booking, scheduling, turnover management. Seamless transitions between rotating staff without gaps or admin burden.
📈 Ownership & Investment in New Facilities
ERS invests in and develops new purpose-built facilities — including the Dawson Creek Hub at $16M construction value. Capital deployed to serve the region long-term, not just manage existing stock.
🎯 Design to Fit the Specific Region & Hospital
Every facility is designed around the specific needs of the hospital and the community it serves. DC Hub is sized for DCDH — the right beds, the right mix, the right location.
Steve's Model — ERS Delivers It

Steve Raper described exactly what NH is looking for: "The hotel model but in longer term leasing." One company, multiple properties, multiple cities, one bill — fully operated, no NH admin burden. That is the ERS foundation layer. Not just beds. A managed, walkable, quality, safe environment where staff want to stay — and choose to go permanent. "If they have a good experience, you want to convert them to full time."

Layer 3
What We Operate — Day to Day
On top of the accommodation foundation, ERS runs the full operating layer. Northern Health doesn't touch any of it.
🧹
Cleaning & Housekeeping
Regular cleaning schedules for all units. Turnover cleaning between rotations. Standards maintained at all times.
🔧
Maintenance & Repairs
All maintenance handled by ERS. No landlord calls, no NH involvement. Issues resolved — full stop.
📅
Owner Schedule Management
Booking coordination, rotation handoffs, vacancy management. Every bed filled, every transition seamless.
✈️
Immigration & Onboarding Support
International healthcare staff supported through immigration documentation and local onboarding. A critical gap ERS fills that traditional housing cannot.
🚌
Transportation Coordination
Local transportation for staff without vehicles. Hospital-adjacent location reduces this burden significantly.
🏘️
Community Connection & Programming
Events, social programming, and community integration. The difference between a bed and a home.
🛡️
Safety & Comfort Standards
All units meet ERS safety and comfort standards. Consistent quality across every bed in every region.
📞
24/7 Staff Coordination
Round-the-clock support. Healthcare staff work around the clock — so does ERS.
